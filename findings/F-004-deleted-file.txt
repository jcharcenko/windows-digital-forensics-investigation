Finding ID: F-004
Title: Temporary File Creation and Deletion
Assessment: Confirmed
Confidence: High

Finding:
The file project-notes.txt was created in the dfiruser Documents directory, written to, and deleted approximately 15 seconds later.

Evidence:
- USN Journal records project-notes.txt FileCreate at 2026-10-05 20:43:41.6285831 UTC.
- Subsequent USN records show DataExtend and Close activity, indicating data was written to the file.
- The USN Journal records FileDelete and Close at 2026-10-05 20:43:56.4463318 UTC.
- The file is associated with MFT entry 34472, sequence 4 in the USN records.
- The corresponding filename was not present in the parsed acquired MFT, and MFTECmd could not locate FILE record 34472-4.
- Autopsy independently identified references to project-notes.txt in the USN Journal, NTFS , and PowerShell ConsoleHost history.
- PowerShell history contains commands corresponding to the creation and removal of project-notes.txt.

Interpretation:
The USN Journal preserves the filesystem lifecycle of project-notes.txt even though the corresponding FILE record was no longer available through the acquired MFT. Correlation with PowerShell history and NTFS  provides additional evidence that the file existed and was subsequently removed.

Limitations:
The contents of project-notes.txt were not recovered. USN Journal and  references establish filesystem activity and filename metadata but should not be interpreted as recovery of the deleted file's contents.
